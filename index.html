    01 · Computational model

    Methamphetamine Perturbations in
    Neurodivergent Reward & Arousal Networks

    Preprint
    Campbell, E. (2026) · Preprint on Zenodo, posted 2026-04-17 · not peer reviewed

    ADHD stimulants and methamphetamine act on the same dopamine machinery, with opposite clinical outcomes. This simulation asks why. It links four dynamical layers, from millisecond dopamine release at the terminal to months of adaptation, and runs them for neurotypical and two ADHD parameter sets.

    Main finding, in the model: temperature is the dominant bifurcation variable for neurotoxicity, ahead of extracellular dopamine. Risk does not climb in a straight line with temperature; past a point the system switches behavior. The model also predicts that ADHD-Combined parameters inflate drug value faster than neurotypical ones, and that ADHD-Inattentive parameters gain more from low therapeutic doses. Its chronic-use tolerance trajectory is consistent with PET imaging data: the comparison targets are Volkow et al. (2001), 24–30% DAT loss in methamphetamine users with about 20% recovery over 12–17 months, and the Ashok et al. (2017) meta-analysis. The paper reports no fit metric for this comparison.

    Weakest point: parameters come from published studies (human PET, rat slice recordings, behavioural data) and the output is compared with group-level findings. It has not been fit to individual human outcome data, and the ADHD-Inattentive locus coeruleus parameters are unconstrained because no recordings exist. This is a model. It is not medical guidance.

    02 · Exploratory model

    Near-Death Experience as a
    Near-Criticality Phenomenon

    Exploratory
    Dynamical-systems experiment · not evidence about real human NDEs

    Verdict: supported only when coherence is measured at long range and restructuring is gated for reproducibility. It fails under naive measures.

    Tests whether Ω = (representational restructuring) / (identity / coherence loss) has an interior maximum along the ridge where the largest Lyapunov exponent λmax ≈ 0 — between order and chaos. A 128-unit rate network was driven across the order-to-chaos transition (Sompolinsky–Crisanti–Sommers gain route), with coherence measured as long-range mutual information of a low-D self-model and restructuring gated for reproducibility.

    Details: the hypothesis fails under naive measures (short-lag coherence, raw perturbation-sensitivity) and only holds once coherence is measured at long temporal range and restructuring is gated for reproducibility against noise. λ≈0 is a local optimum of structured reorganization with retained coherence — whether it is the global optimum depends on exactly those two assumptions, which are defensible but not free.
