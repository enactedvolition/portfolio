01 · Computational model

Methamphetamine Perturbations in
Neurodivergent Reward & Arousal Networks

Preprint
Campbell, E. (2026) · Preprint on Zenodo · status [CONFIRM: "In submission" here vs "Published · iterating" on the main site; which is current, and where is it submitted?]

ADHD stimulants and methamphetamine act on the same dopamine machinery, with opposite clinical outcomes. This simulation asks why. It links six layers, from millisecond dopamine release at the terminal to months of adaptation, and runs them for neurotypical and two ADHD parameter sets.

Main finding, in the model: temperature is the dominant bifurcation variable for neurotoxicity, ahead of extracellular dopamine. Risk does not climb in a straight line with temperature; past a point the system switches behavior. The model also predicts that ADHD-Combined parameters inflate drug value faster than neurotypical ones, and that ADHD-Inattentive parameters gain more from low therapeutic doses. It reproduces the chronic-use tolerance curve reported in PET imaging [CONFIRM: which PET study, and what fit metric?].

02 · Exploratory model

Near-Death Experience as a
Near-Criticality Phenomenon

Exploratory
Dynamical-systems experiment · not evidence about real human NDEs

Verdict: supported only when coherence is measured at long range and restructuring is gated for reproducibility. It fails under naive measures.

Tests whether Ω = (representational restructuring) / (identity / coherence loss) has an interior maximum along the ridge where the largest Lyapunov exponent λmax ≈ 0 — between order and chaos. A 128-unit rate network was driven across the order-to-chaos transition (Sompolinsky–Crisanti–Sommers gain route), with coherence measured as long-range mutual information of a low-D self-model and restructuring gated for reproducibility.
